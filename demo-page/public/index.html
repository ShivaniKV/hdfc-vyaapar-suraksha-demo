Section I – Property Damage: two kinds

Named Perils

Pays only for the dangers listed in the policy: fire, explosion, lightning, landslide, forest fire, impact damage, riot and strike, water tank bursting, sprinkler leakage. You can add storm and flood and earthquake.

All Risk

Pays for any sudden, accidental physical loss or damage, except what the policy specifically excludes. It covers more situations.
