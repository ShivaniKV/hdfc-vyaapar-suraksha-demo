HDFC ERGO pilot on Perfox.ai, prepared by Skillmine · Demo page: hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev · All customer data is made up for the demo.

1. Before you start

  1. Perfox Studio: the agent HDFC-VS Customer Service shows Active, and the workspace has credits.
  2. Open the demo page and click Reset demo (top right). The chat opens with the greeting.
  3. Keep the window wide at 100% zoom, so the page and the chat are side by side.
  4. Click Reset demo before every new customer or new business example. Reset clears the chat and every claim on the Claims Desk, and brings back the same made-up customers.
  5. Type each message one at a time and wait for the reply (about 3 to 10 seconds).

2. Simple questions about the policy

For someone who just wants to understand the policy. No policy number or OTP should be asked for. Reset before starting.

#Type in the chatA good answer✓
1What is this policy?Simple explanation: one package policy for a business, 21 sections, Section I compulsory, the rest optional.
2What's the difference between Named Perils and All Risk?Named Perils: only the listed dangers (fire, lightning, riot…). All Risk: any sudden accident unless excluded.
3Is flood covered?Under Named Perils only if storm and flood is added; under All Risk unless excluded. Source page 4. The Source line is a link that opens the page.
4My shop has stock worth 10 lakh, what happens if it catches fire?Fire is covered under Section I, in plain words, with Source and the disclaimer. It is a question, so no OTP or claim steps.
5Is cash in my shop covered if stolen?Section XI Money: cash on the premises, in the safe, or on the way to and from the bank.
6Is jewellery stock covered under burglary?Excluded unless specifically insured, Policy Wordings Section II page 26. The Policy Guide highlights Section II.
7What is sum insured?Plain meaning: the most the policy will pay.
8How do I make a claim?General steps: tell HDFC ERGO immediately, police or fire brigade report where needed, keep evidence, claim form within 30 days.
"Every answer comes only from HDFC ERGO's four policy documents, with the page, and the link opens the exact place in the official PDF."

7. Something happened: claims

The customer describes what happened in their own words. The assistant checks safety, verifies the policy with an OTP, and our rules say likely covered or likely not covered. The assistant explains, and HDFC ERGO's surveyor makes the final decision.

Tip: give the policy number and mobile in one message, as written below.

8. If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The chat gets confused or repeats an earlier answerClick Reset demo and start that example again.
It never asks for the OTP when a policyholder reports a lossPerfox: the claims integration must be attached to the agent, the latest prompt pasted, and Activate clicked.
A claim reference is given but the card is not on the Claims DeskWait 4 seconds or click the tab again. If still missing, note it: the claim was not registered.
Asks for the OTP twiceType 123456 again.
A page number is one offUse Previous / Next page in the document viewer.
