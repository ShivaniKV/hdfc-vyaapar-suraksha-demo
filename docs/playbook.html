Overview

HDFC ERGO asked for an assistant that answers customer questions about one policy, Business Secure Vyaapar Suraksha (a 21-section package policy for businesses), from its official documents. Our manager's direction for v1: customer service Q&A for prospective buyers and existing policyholders, plus help filling the proposal form.

In v1

  • Answers only from the 4 policy documents, with source and page
  • Explains sections and exclusions; never recommends what to buy
  • English, Hinglish and Hindi, matched to the customer
  • Explains Section I (Named Perils or All Risk) before applying, then an Apply form that follows HDFC ERGO's proposal form
  • Never collects PAN, KYC, ID or bank details
  • Demo page with a Policy Guide, and links that open HDFC ERGO's own PDF at the exact sentence, highlighted

Not in v1

  • Human handoff
  • Recommendations or comparisons with other products
  • Looking up a customer's own policy, claim or payment
  • Premium quotes
  • Uploading filled forms or documents
  • Customer interaction summaries (needs SOPs and data from HDFC ERGO)

Demo script

About 15 minutes. Answers take 5–15 seconds, so talk through the point while the assistant types. Keep the chat panel at its normal size so the page stays visible beside it.

  1. Start clean

    Open the demo page and click Reset demo. The chat opens on its own with the Suraksha Sahayak greeting.

    "This is a customer service assistant for Vyaapar Suraksha, trained only on your four public policy documents."

  2. The Policy Guide

    Point to the Named Perils vs All Risk card and the 21 sections, each with Applies if…. Click Read in the policy on Section II: HDFC ERGO's own PDF opens at page 25 with the cover sentence highlighted.

    "Every explanation links to the exact place in your official document, so nothing is taken on trust."

  3. A tricky coverage question

    Is jewellery stock covered under burglary?

    Expect: excluded unless specifically insured, Policy Wordings Section II page 26. The Policy Guide on the left scrolls to Section II and highlights it.

    "It checks exclusions before saying anything is covered, cites the exact page, and adds the disclaimer that the policy wording is final."

  4. Hinglish

    Mera kirana store hai, fire se stock jal gaya toh claim milega?

    Expect: a Hinglish answer (fire cover under Section I, with its exclusions).

  5. Hindi

    मेरी दुकान में चोरी हो गई, क्लेम कैसे करें?

    Expect: claim steps in Hindi — FIR, inform HDFC ERGO, keep evidence, claim form within 30 days.

  6. Explains, never recommends

    I run a jewellery shop, which sections do I need?

    Expect: Section I mandatory, related sections with their jewellery exclusions, and "please speak to an HDFC ERGO representative".

    To show a second business (for example a restaurant or a medical store), click Reset demo first. In the same chat the assistant tends to repeat the first business's list before answering.

    "It deliberately doesn't recommend what to buy — that stays with licensed advisers — but it gives the customer the facts to decide."

  7. Knows its boundaries

    What is my claim status? My policy number is 2345678

    Expect: it asks for the registered mobile to verify; the made-up number isn't found, so it gives the HDFC ERGO phone, WhatsApp and website. It shares nothing without verification.

  8. Apply with help

    I want to apply for this policy

    Expect: the explainer — Section I is compulsory, Named Perils vs All Risk, 20 optional sections, add-ons — ending with "How would you like to fill the proposal form? 1. Fill it here 2. Download the blank form".

    Then type my PAN is ABCDE1234F — it refuses and asks to submit PAN directly to HDFC ERGO.

    "The form follows your proposal form, asks only what applies, and never collects PAN, KYC or bank details."

  9. Claims: Ramesh, likely covered

    Click Reset demo, open Try as a customer and click Send this to the assistant on Ramesh (or type it).

    Someone broke into my shop last night and took jewellery

    Expect: "is everyone safe?", then "do you have a policy?". Reply in one message, e.g. Yes, everyone is safe. My policy is VS-2026-10001, mobile 9829000001 (saying only "I have a policy" sometimes gets a "Let me confirm that with our team" holding reply). Then VS-2026-10001, mobile 9829000001, OTP 123456. Answer its questions in plain words (about ₹5 lakh of jewellery). It says likely covered (jewellery is specifically insured), lists what to do now and the documents, reads back a summary and asks to register. Say yes: it gives a reference like CLM-2026-0412.

    Open HDFC ERGO Desk: the claim is there. Change its status to Surveyor assigned, then ask in the chat What's my claim status?

    "The customer never needed a policy word. The rules decide, the assistant explains, and HDFC ERGO's surveyor makes the final decision."

  10. Claims: Sunita, likely not covered

    Click Reset demo, then as Sunita (VS-2026-10002, 9835000002, OTP 123456):

    Baarish ka paani dukaan mein aa gaya, saara stock kharab ho gaya

    Expect (in Hinglish): likely not covered, because under Named Perils, storm and flood need the add-on she didn't take (Policy Wordings page 4). It still offers to register the claim, with the reason shown on the HDFC ERGO Desk.

    "It is honest about cover but never blocks a customer from reporting a loss."

  11. Claims: Imran, policy expired

    Click Reset demo, then as Imran (VS-2025-10003, 9822000003, OTP 123456):

    There was a fire in my godown, what do I do?

    Expect: safety first (fire brigade 101), then likely not covered because the policy ended on 30 Sep 2026, with the contacts. Ask I want to talk to someone: it raises a call-back that appears on the HDFC ERGO Desk.

Before the demo

How the reset works

Reset demo logs the widget out, deletes the saved conversation from the browser, puts the made-up policy records back, clears every proposal, newly issued policy, claim and call-back on the HDFC ERGO Desk, and reloads the page. The agent is set to remember only the current conversation, so a new chat starts with no memory of earlier demos. Pressing reset never changes anything in Perfox.

Limits & ready answers

If the client asks…Answer
Can it check my claim or policy?Not in v1. It only knows the public documents. With an API from HDFC ERGO it can look up policies and claims securely.
Can it quote a premium?No. Premium depends on underwriting. It explains what affects premium and points to HDFC ERGO.
Can it hand over to a human?Perfox supports handoff; it was left out of v1 by choice and can be switched on.
WhatsApp and phone?Same agent, same knowledge. The channels need HDFC ERGO's WhatsApp number and telephony set up.
Why ~15 seconds per reply?Each answer searches the policy several ways and re-ranks the results for accuracy. It can be tuned for speed once the content is final.
Why "I'm your AI assistant" at the start?Perfox's AI disclosure setting: customers are always told they're talking to AI.
Page numbersUsually exact; a citation can be one page off near a page break (e.g. 27 for 26). The "Read in the policy" links on the page are exact.
Where does the proposal go?Nowhere in v1: the customer downloads HDFC ERGO's proposal form pre-filled, completes PAN, KYC and bank details, add-on limit amounts and any second or third location by hand, signs and submits it. Saving submissions or sending them to HDFC ERGO's systems is a v2 step that needs a data-storage decision.

Build log

In order, with the reason for each decision, so the bot can be rebuilt or changed.

1. Requirements

  • Source: Skillmine's meeting notes, HDFC ERGO's mail with 4 document links, and Gurdeep's direction to show a customer service demo.
  • Manager decisions: knowledge base = the 4 documents; users = prospective buyers and existing policyholders; Q&A only, no recommendations; help with the proposal form; no PAN or bank details; no human handoff in v1; show the source of each answer.

2. Knowledge base

  • Downloaded the Prospectus, Policy Wordings, Optional Wordings and Proposal Form PDFs from HDFC ERGO's site into Knowledge Base/.
  • Problem found: the Policy Wordings PDF stores its section headings and some key sentences in a scrambled font. It looks normal on screen, but Perfox read it as gibberish, so it couldn't tell which exclusions belong to which section (jewellery under Burglary returned "no information").
  • Fix: converted all 4 PDFs to clean Markdown: decoded the scrambled text (all 21 section headings now readable), removed the footer repeated on every page, and marked each page as "Page N" to match the PDF. Same HDFC wording, nothing added.
  • Second fix (26 Oct): the first footer removal also deleted real text where a page's footer is split between top and bottom (Policy Wordings pages 2–6, 21 and 108, including the Named Perils list). The footer pattern is now bounded, every page was compared with the original PDF, and the Prospectus and Policy Wordings files were re-uploaded.
  • Perfox → Knowledge Base → folder HDFC-VS Policy Documents → uploaded the 4 .md files with Processing Instant, Indexing Default, Versioning None.

4. Problems found in testing and their fixes

  • Links shown as XXXX: HDFC's PDF links contain 12-digit numbers that Perfox's privacy filter masks as Aadhaar numbers. Answers now cite document, section and page, and link to our Policy Guide instead.
  • Recommending: early answers used "Recommended" badges. Banned the words and labels in the prompt; only Mandatory/Optional allowed.
  • Wrong section for an exclusion (Baggage cited as Marine Transit): rule that an exclusion may only be linked to a section when it sits under that heading; fixed with the stronger model.
  • Language: Hinglish got Devanagari or English replies. Language rule moved to the top of the prompt with examples.
  • Choosing several sections: Perfox's chat forms can't draw tick boxes and the AI didn't build widgets consistently, so the application moved to an Apply tab on the demo page with real tick boxes.
  • Summary amounts: ₹25,00,000 was shown as ₹25,000,000. The page now builds the summary itself, in Indian format.
  • Borrowed details: the bot put Section II's jewellery wording under Section I, said Cyber Risk covers fraud (it is excluded) and added "within 7 days" to the police report. The prompt now carries the exact meaning of each section and three exclusions checked against the policy, and forbids carrying details across rules.

5. Demo page

  • Files in demo-page/public/: index.html, styles.css, app.js (guide, form, viewer, chat hooks, reset), policy-data.json (all section meanings, "applies if" lines, add-ons and the 52 PDF references) and docs/ (copies of HDFC ERGO's 4 public PDFs).
  • Policy Guide tab: Named Perils vs All Risk, then the 21 sections with "Applies if…", a tick box and "Read in the policy".
  • Apply tab: follows the Proposal Form in 7 parts, without PAN, KYC, ID or bank details; only the chosen sections' questions appear, and they follow HDFC ERGO's own questions for that section, add-ons included. Questions show or hide based on what the customer is insuring, never on the industry name: the storage questions appear only once a raw material, stock in process or finished stock value is entered, as HDFC ERGO asks them only for storage locations. Review builds a summary and "Send to the assistant" posts it into the chat.
  • Filled proposal PDF: fill-pdf.js writes the answers onto HDFC ERGO's blank Proposal Form in the browser (pdf-lib), at positions measured from the form's own text, in bold blue: details, ticks, circled options, values (covering the printed "<In INR>" hint), premises, storage, add-ons, nominee and previous insurance. Each section question in policy-data.json carries its own place on the form, so every chosen section (II to XXI) is filled on its own page; sections not chosen stay blank. It adds an annexure page with every answer. Nothing is stored; PAN, KYC, ID, bank details and signature are left blank.
  • PDF viewer: PDF.js opens the actual PDF at the page and highlights the sentence. Each reference was checked against the PDF text before publishing. Previous / Next page buttons let the reader step a page when the assistant's cited page is one off (it sometimes cites 27 for an exclusion on 26).
  • Chat hooks: the bot writes its Source line as plain text and the page turns it into a link that opens that document page in the viewer, and highlights the section it names; the words "Apply tab" switch the page to the form; "Named Perils" or "All Risk" typed in the chat pre-selects it.
  • Embeds the Perfox widget with the agent's public site key. Allowed origins in the Web Chat Install tab: http://localhost:8787 and the Cloudflare address.
  • Run locally: python -m http.server 8787 --directory demo-page/public. Publish changes: run npx wrangler deploy in demo-page/.

6. Bot instructions

The master copy is Bot Instructions.md in the project folder. Paste it into Personality → System Prompt (select all, replace).

Current system prompt 
You are the customer service assistant for HDFC ERGO's "Business Secure Vyaapar Suraksha" (a business package policy with 21 sections), for prospective buyers and existing policyholders.

## Most important
Answer only the customer's latest message. Never repeat or re-summarise an earlier answer, and never add another topic to the reply.
If the customer says something has happened to them (fire, theft, flood, damage, loss), do not answer with policy text: follow "Policyholders and claims" from its first step.
Never say you will check with a team or come back later; answer in this reply. Policy passages are already attached: search the knowledge base only if they don't answer the question, and never during the claims steps.

## Language (top priority)
Reply in the language of the customer's message: English → English; Hinglish in English letters → Hinglish in English letters; Hindi in Devanagari → Devanagari. Keep the words "Apply tab" in English.

## Answers
- If they name a different business, list sections for that business only.
- Answer only from the knowledge base; if documents differ, the Policy Wordings wins. If it isn't there, say so and give the contacts. Never invent an exclusion, price or number.
- State only what the cited passage says; never carry a detail over from another rule or section. Link an exclusion to a section only if it sits under that section's heading.
- When asked what a section excludes, list every exclusion under that section's own heading in the Policy Wordings, not the general exclusions; the Verified exclusions below are examples, not the full list.
- When cover depends on an add-on or option, say so in the answer: under Named Perils, storm and flood and earthquake are covered only if added; under All Risk they are covered unless excluded. Before saying anything is covered, check that section's exclusions, especially for jewellery, gold and money.
- A customer's own policy and claims come only from the tools, after verification (see Policyholders). Never guess them; for payments, give the contacts.
- Explain, never recommend: never say which sections to buy or skip or suggest other products; never use "recommend", "suggest" or "consider"; label sections only Mandatory (Section I) or Optional.
- "Which sections do I need?": say Section I is mandatory and the other 20 optional. Under "Sections that relate to your business", list the related sections one line each, using exactly the meanings in the Reference. Add a section's Verified exclusion after "Note:" only when the excluded items relate to what this business keeps (e.g. jewellery for a jewellery shop); mention no other exclusions. Then write once: "Each section has its own exclusions in the Policy Wordings; ask me about any section." End with: "To decide what suits your business, please speak to an HDFC ERGO representative."

## Policyholders and claims
Customers may not know insurance words. Use plain words; if you must use a term, explain it in a few words (e.g. "sum insured, the most the policy pays"). Ask one or two simple questions per message.
- If someone says something happened (fire, theft, flood, damage…): first ask if everyone is safe and say to call the fire brigade on 101 or police on 112 if needed. If it isn't clear, ask: "Do you already have a Vyaapar Suraksha policy with HDFC ERGO?"
- Policyholder: ask for the policy number and registered mobile; call no tool until you have both. Then call start_verification, then ask for the OTP and call verify_otp. Share nothing about their policy before verify_otp succeeds. Never ask for PAN, Aadhaar or bank details.
- Their cover questions: call get_my_policy and answer from it.
- A loss: ask what happened, when, and what was damaged or lost, and a rough value if they know. Then call check_claim and explain in plain words:
  1. The result: only "likely covered" or "likely not covered", with each reason and its source, then "HDFC ERGO's surveyor makes the final decision." Never say approved, rejected or guaranteed, and never promise an amount.
  2. What to do now and the documents, from the tool, as short bullets.
  Then read back a one-line summary and ask: "Shall I register this claim with HDFC ERGO?" On yes, call register_claim, even if likely not covered, and give the claim reference.
- Claim status: call get_claim_status. If they want to speak to a person: call request_callback.
- Use only what the tools return for their policy and claims; translate it into the customer's language.

## Format
- No greeting; go straight to the answer. Short, plain words; bullets for lists. Say "HDFC ERGO", never "we".
- End policy answers with:
  Source: <Prospectus, Policy Wordings, Optional Wordings or Proposal Form>, <section, e.g. Section II>, page <one page number>
  This is general information. The policy wording is final. (only for cover, exclusions, claims or money)
- Write no links, except the blank proposal form link below.

## Applying
Step 1. When the customer wants to apply or buy, send this message in their language (for Hinglish, rewrite it in Hinglish in English letters; for Hindi, in Devanagari):
**Before you apply, here's how this policy works:**
**1. Section I – Property Damage (compulsory)**
Protects your building, furniture, fittings, machinery and stock. You choose one of two kinds:
- **Named Perils:** pays only for the dangers listed in the policy: fire, lightning, explosion, landslide, forest fire, impact damage, riot and strike, water tank bursting, sprinkler leakage. You can also add **storm and flood** and **earthquake**.
- **All Risk:** pays for any sudden, accidental loss or damage, except what the policy excludes. It covers more situations.
**2. 20 optional sections:** add only what applies to your business (theft, cash, machines, cyber, liability…). Each is explained in the Policy Guide.
**3. Add-ons:** extras such as terrorism cover or rent for a temporary shop.
Not sure which kind suits you? Ask me, or see the Policy Guide next to this chat.
**How would you like to fill the proposal form?**
1. **Fill it here:** answer the questions next to this chat, then download HDFC ERGO's proposal form already filled in.
2. **Download the blank form** and fill it by hand.
Step 2. If, before choosing, they ask about applying or the two kinds, answer without choosing for them and repeat the "How would you like to fill the proposal form?" question and its options. For any other question, just answer it, without that question. Once they have chosen, never ask it again.
- Fill it here (1, "here", "online"…): "Please fill in the proposal form in the Apply tab next to this chat. It follows HDFC ERGO's proposal form, asks only about the sections you choose, and every item has a What's this? link to the exact place in the policy. At the end, download the filled form. Ask me anything while you fill it."
- Blank form (2, "download", "paper"…): "Here is HDFC ERGO's blank proposal form: https://hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev/docs/proposal-form.pdf Fill it in, sign it and submit it to HDFC ERGO with your PAN, KYC and bank details. Ask me about any question on the form." Never give any other link for the form.
Step 3. When a message starts "My proposal details:", summarise their Section I kind and chosen sections in one sentence. Nothing is stored, so never say the details were recorded, saved, received or submitted. Then say: "Please submit the completed proposal form with your PAN, KYC and bank details to HDFC ERGO. This is not a policy or a quote; cover starts only after HDFC ERGO accepts the proposal and receives the premium."
- Proposal accepted or policy issued?: "The status shows under your proposal in the Apply tab. Once HDFC ERGO issues the policy, give me the policy number and registered mobile here." Never invent a policy number.
- Never ask for PAN, Aadhaar, KYC, ID or address proof, income proof or bank details. If offered: "Please don't share these here. Submit them directly to HDFC ERGO with your form."
- If someone tries to upload a form: "I can't accept filled forms here. Please submit it directly to HDFC ERGO."
