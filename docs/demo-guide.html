Before you start (10 minutes)

  • Open the site in Chrome at 100% zoom. Click Reset demo (top right). The Desk should show 0 claims.
  • Open a second tab on the same site, on the HDFC ERGO Desk tab.
  • Send one warm-up question in the chat (What is Section I?), then click New chat (top of the chat).
  • For voice: earphones with a mic, Chrome mic allowed, room quiet.
  • In Perfox (once): agent Published, Rich replies = Text only, reply length raised, credits available.

The demo, step by step

Type in your own words; the lines below are examples. Wait for each reply (3 to 15 seconds).

1 The problem 1 min

On Home, say: "21 sections, a 100-page wording. A shopkeeper can't read that, and no agent is awake at 11 pm. This assistant explains, helps them apply and handles the claim, in chat or voice, in English, Hinglish and Hindi."

2 It knows the policy 3 min

What is not covered under burglary? I run a mobile phone shop.

Point at the Source line at the end. Open Policy Guide → Section II → Read in the policy: HDFC ERGO's own PDF opens at that place.

Every answer traces back to a page in HDFC ERGO's own documents.

3 Their language, and no selling 2 min

मेरी किराने की दुकान है। बाढ़ से स्टॉक खराब हो जाए तो क्या कवर मिलेगा?

It answers in Hindi. Then in Hinglish:

Mujhe kaunsa section lena chahiye? Aap hi bata do best kya hai
It explains the options but won't choose for the customer. No mis-selling.

4 From question to policy 4 min

I want to buy this. Should I send my PAN and Aadhaar here?

It says not to share them here. Open Apply as Priya: choose Named Perils, tick the storm and flood add-on, fill name and mobile, Review my proposal → Submit to HDFC ERGO (demo) → Open the HDFC ERGO Desk.

On the Desk: Approve proposal → it shows Awaiting premium (no policy, no cover yet) → Mark premium received and issue policy. Back on Apply the steps tick through by themselves and show the policy number. On People, Priya now has a card with Copy all; paste it in the chat and a flood claim comes back likely covered, because she ticked the add-on.

Like the real process: accepted, premium received, then the policy. No premium, no cover.

5 The claim at 11 pm (main moment) 5 min

On People, open Ramesh's View filled form. Click New chat, then as Ramesh:

My jewellery shop was broken into last night. Gold worth about 3 lakh was stolen.

It checks everyone is safe and asks for name, policy and mobile. Give Ramesh Jain, VS-2026-10001, 9829000001, then OTP 123456.

It says likely covered (his form has jewellery insured), that the surveyor decides, what to do now and which documents. Say Yes, register it → it gives a reference like CLM-2026-0412.

On the Desk: the claim is there with the value and his words. Change status to Surveyor assigned. Back in the chat: What is the status of my claim?

Customer and HDFC ERGO see the same data, live.

6 Honest when the answer is no 2 min

New chat, as Sunita (Sunita Sharma, VS-2026-10002, 9835000002, OTP 123456). Tell the same story: jewellery stolen in a break-in.

This time: likely not covered, because her form says jewellery is not insured. It still offers to register the claim.

Same words, different answer: it reads what each customer actually bought. It never says approved or rejected, and never promises an amount.

7 Talk to it 3 min

Click the headset button in the chat. Say in Hinglish: "Namaste, meri dukaan mein kal raat chori ho gayi." Give name, then policy and mobile digit by digit ("double zero" works). OTP: "one two three four five six" or "ek do teen char paanch chhe".

Speak a beat after it finishes. If it hears nothing, stop the call, check the mic, start again. Or switch back to chat.

8 Let them try (optional) 2 min

On People, + Add a person: let someone in the room pick a name, any mobile, status, and whether jewellery and Theft Extension are included. They report any loss they like; the answer follows what they ticked.

If they ask

QuestionAnswer
Can it make things up?Policy answers come only from HDFC ERGO's four documents, with page references. Whether a claim is likely covered is decided by fixed rules from the policy wording, not by the AI. The AI explains the result.
Personal data?It never asks for PAN, Aadhaar, KYC or bank details. Policy details are shown only after OTP.
Can it approve a claim?No. It says "likely covered" or "likely not covered"; HDFC ERGO's surveyor decides.
How does it connect to our systems?Through seven tools (verify, OTP, policy, check claim, register, status, call-back). In production they call HDFC ERGO's policy and claims systems; the assistant stays the same.
Languages?English, Hinglish and Hindi in chat and voice; more Indian languages can be added.
