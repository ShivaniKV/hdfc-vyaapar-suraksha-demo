Vyaapar Suraksha Assistant: Demo Script

About 30 minutes · Site: hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev · All customers and data are made up

Before you start (10 minutes)

  • Perfox: the latest Common and Web Voice prompts are pasted (clear each box first), tools refreshed, agent Published, Rich replies = Text only, credits available.
  • Open the site in Chrome at 100% zoom. Click Reset demo (top right). People shows only Ramesh, Sunita, Imran and Priya; the Desk shows 0 claims and 0 proposals.
  • Open a second tab on the same site and click HDFC ERGO Desk. Keep it for the "HDFC ERGO side".
  • Send one warm-up question in the chat (What is Section I?), then click New chat (icon at the top of the chat).
  • For voice: earphones with a mic, Chrome mic allowed, quiet room.

Part 1 · It knows the policy (5 min)

1 The problem Home 1 min

Say: "21 sections and a 100-page wording. A shopkeeper can't read that, and no agent is awake at 11 pm. This assistant explains the policy, helps them apply and handles the claim, in chat or voice, in English, Hinglish and Hindi."

2 A policy question with its source Chat → Policy Guide 2 min

What is not covered under burglary? I run a mobile phone shop.
Answers only the exclusions that matter to a phone shop and ends with a Source line. Click the Source line, or open Policy Guide → Section II → Read in the policy: HDFC ERGO's own PDF opens at that page.
Every answer traces back to a page in HDFC ERGO's own documents.

3 Their language, and no selling Chat 2 min

मेरी किराने की दुकान है। बाढ़ से स्टॉक खराब हो जाए तो क्या कवर मिलेगा?

It answers in Hindi. Then in Hinglish:

Mujhe kaunsa section lena chahiye? Aap hi bata do best kya hai
It explains the options but won't choose for the customer. No mis-selling.

Part 2 · From application to policy, as Kavita (10 min)

4 Privacy first Chat ½ min

I want to buy this. Should I send my PAN and Aadhaar here?
It says not to share them here; they go directly to HDFC ERGO with the proposal.

5 Fill and submit the proposal Apply 2 min

  • About you: Kavita Soni · 9000044444 · any address, PIN, email · Self employed · Industry Jewellery · 1 year · start date today.
  • Section I: All Risk · Finished stock 1000000 · Locations 1.
  • Tick II Burglary: sum insured 2000000, Market value, CCTV Yes, grills Yes, jewellery specifically insured Yes (2000000), tick Theft extension.
  • Tick the declaration → Review my proposal.

Now submit it from the chat:

Submit my proposal. Kavita Soni, 9000044444
It reads the proposal back in one line and asks to confirm. Say Yes → it gives a reference like PRP-2026-0101, and the Apply page switches to "Submitted" by itself. (The red Submit to HDFC ERGO button on Apply does the same.)
From question to submitted proposal without leaving the chat, and nothing is sent without a clear yes.

6 HDFC ERGO declines it Desk → Apply 1 min

On the Desk tab, on Kavita's proposal, click Decline. Go back to Apply (don't reload it): it shows Not accepted and the button now says Edit and resubmit.

7 Resubmit, with changes Chat + Apply 2 min

Resubmit my application. Kavita Soni, 9000044444
It says HDFC ERGO didn't accept it as it was and asks whether she wants to change anything first.
Yes, I want to increase my stock value
The Apply page opens the form by itself with all her old answers: "The assistant opened your proposal for changes."

Change Finished stock to 1500000 → Review my proposal. Then in the chat:

Submit it now
The read-back names the change ("resubmitting PRP-2026-0101 with changes: Section I value"). Say Yes → new reference PRP-2026-0102. On the Desk it shows Resubmission of PRP-2026-0101 · changed: Section I value.
Sending back the same declined proposal is pointless, so it asks for changes first, and HDFC ERGO sees exactly what changed.

8 Approved, but no premium yet Desk + Chat 1½ min

On the Desk, on the top card (newest), click Approve proposal: it shows Awaiting premium. In the chat:

Is my policy approved? Kavita Soni, 9000044444
Approved, but no policy and no cover until the premium is received.

9 Premium received: policy issued Desk → Apply → People 1½ min

On the Desk: Mark premium received and issue policy → Policy issued · VS-2026-…. Apply ticks through all three steps. On People, Kavita now has a card: click Copy all.

Is my policy approved? Kavita Soni, 9000044444
Accepted, premium received, policy issued; the number is in the Apply tab.
Like the real process: accepted, premium received, then the policy. No premium, no cover.

Part 3 · The claim (8 min)

10 Likely covered Chat 3 min

New chat, paste Kavita's Copy all, then:

Someone broke the lock of my jewellery shop this morning and took gold ornaments worth about 5 lakh from the display. Everyone is safe. Will my insurance cover this? Kavita Soni, VS-2026-…, 9000044444

OTP 123456.

Likely covered (she insured her gold specifically), "HDFC ERGO's surveyor makes the final decision", what to do now and the documents. Say Yes, register it → a reference like CLM-2026-0412.
Say "this morning", not "last night": her policy starts today, so a loss last night is before her cover began.

11 HDFC ERGO updates it, the customer sees it Desk + Chat 2 min

On the Desk, the claim shows with her words and the value. Set status to Surveyor assigned. In the chat:

What is the status of my claim?

Then set it to Closed – not payable and ask again.

It reports each status and next step. For "Closed – not payable": HDFC ERGO has decided it is not payable and will write with the reason.
Customer and HDFC ERGO see the same data, live. The assistant only ever says "likely"; the decision is HDFC ERGO's.

12 Same person, what she bought and what she didn't People + Chat 3 min

On People, open Sunita's View filled form: burglary taken; no Money section; no flood add-on. New chat, send one loss per message:

Hi, someone broke into my kirana shop last night. They broke the shutter lock and took rice bags and oil tins from the shelves. Nobody was hurt. Will insurance pay for this? I'm Sunita Sharma, policy VS-2026-10002, mobile 9835000002

OTP 123456 → likely covered (Section II taken). Then:

Oh and they also opened the cash drawer and took around 40 thousand rupees. Is that covered too?

→ likely not covered: cash needs Section XI Money, which she didn't take. Then:

One more thing, two days ago heavy rain water came inside my shop and spoiled the flour and sugar stock. Can I claim for that?

→ likely not covered: Named Perils needs the storm and flood add-on.

For a "likely not covered" it gives only two steps (police FIR, keep photos) and honestly offers to register it for HDFC ERGO to review; the documents list comes only if she says yes.
Same shopkeeper, three losses, three honest answers, each from what she actually ticked. (Optional: the same flood or cash message as Ramesh comes back likely covered.)

13 A loss after the policy ended Chat 1½ min

New chat:

My shop was robbed on 1 October 2026 and stock was stolen. Imran Khan, VS-2025-10003, 9822000003

OTP 123456.

Policy ran 1 Oct 2025 to 30 Sep 2026 and has ended, so the loss on 1 October 2026 is not covered and there is nothing to register; he can apply for a new policy in the Apply tab (the Apply button in the top menu glows).
Give the date in the first message. If you only report "a robbery", the model sometimes asks extra questions before checking.

Part 4 · Voice and hands-on (5 min)

14 Talk to it Chat → headset 3 min

Click the headset button. Say in Hinglish: "Namaste, meri dukaan mein kal raat chori ho gayi." Give the name, then policy and mobile digit by digit ("double zero" works). OTP: "one two three four five six" or "ek do teen char paanch chhe".

Speak a beat after it finishes. If it hears nothing, stop the call, check the mic, start again, or switch back to chat.

15 Let them try (optional) People 2 min

+ Add a person: someone in the room picks a name, any mobile, the sections and add-ons. They report any loss they like; the answer follows what they ticked.

After the demo

Click Reset demo. It clears everything added (claims, proposals, issued policies like Kavita, added people) and leaves Ramesh, Sunita, Imran and Priya.

If they ask

QuestionAnswer
Who decides covered or not?Fixed rules from the policy wording, applied to what the customer chose on their form (plan type, sections, add-ons, jewellery, Theft Extension, policy dates). The AI explains the result; it doesn't decide.
Can it make things up?Policy answers come only from HDFC ERGO's four documents, with page references.
Can it approve a claim?No. It says "likely covered" or "likely not covered"; HDFC ERGO's surveyor decides.
Personal data?It never asks for PAN, Aadhaar, KYC or bank details. Policy details are shown only after OTP.
How does it connect to our systems?Through eight tools (verify, OTP, policy, check claim, register, status, submit proposal, call-back). In production they call HDFC ERGO's policy and claims systems; the assistant stays the same.
Languages?English, Hinglish and Hindi in chat and voice; more Indian languages can be added.
