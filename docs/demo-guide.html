Vyaapar Suraksha Assistant: Demo Guide

How to run a 20-minute demo for HDFC ERGO that shows the assistant answering policy questions, taking a proposal and handling a claim, in chat and voice, in three languages.

Site: hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev Agent: Perfox Studio, hdfc-ergo-demo All customer data is made up

The story in one line

"A shopkeeper's shop is broken into at 11 at night. Today they wait for office hours. With this assistant, they get a clear, honest answer in their own language within two minutes, the claim is registered, and HDFC ERGO's team sees it on their desk instantly."

Everything in the demo supports that story: the assistant explains and never sells, it never asks for PAN, KYC or bank details, and whether a loss is likely covered is decided by fixed rules taken from the policy wording, not by the AI's guess. The AI explains the result.

30 minutes before

  1. Perfox Studio: the agent is Published, the Vyaapar Suraksha MCP integration is attached to the AI Agent node, and the workspace has credits.
  2. Open the site in Chrome at 100% zoom on a wide screen. Click Reset (top right). This clears claims, call-backs and proposals; people you added stay.
  3. Open a second tab on the HDFC ERGO Desk tab of the same site. If you have two screens, put the Desk on the screen the audience sees.
  4. Voice: plug in earphones with a microphone, allow the microphone in Chrome, and set Windows microphone level to 100% with Audio enhancements off.
  5. Warm-up: send one question in the chat (for example What is Section I?) so the first reply in front of the audience is fast. Then click New chat in the chat header.
  6. Keep this guide open on your phone or a printout.

The demo, act by act

Type each message once and wait for the reply (3 to 15 seconds). If a reply looks cut off, click New chat and ask again.

1 Open with the problem 1 min

Start on the Home tab.

"Vyaapar Suraksha has 21 sections, a 100-page wording and two kinds of property cover. A shopkeeper can't read that, and an agent can't be awake at 11 at night. This assistant explains the policy, helps them apply and handles their claim, in chat or by voice, in English, Hinglish and Hindi."

2 It knows the policy, and shows where it read it 3 min

Type:

What is not covered under burglary? I run a mobile phone shop.

Point at the Source line (Policy Wordings, Section II, page 26). Then open the Policy Guide tab, find Section II and click Read in the policy: HDFC ERGO's own PDF opens at that place, highlighted.

Every answer is traceable to a page in HDFC ERGO's own documents. Compliance teams care about this most.

3 It speaks the customer's language, and doesn't sell 2 min

मेरी किराने की दुकान है। बाढ़ से स्टॉक खराब हो जाए तो क्या कवर मिलेगा?

It answers in Hindi script and explains that flood needs an add-on under Named Perils. Then:

Mujhe kaunsa section lena chahiye? Aap hi bata do best kya hai
"It explains the options in Hinglish but won't choose for the customer. That decision stays with the customer or an HDFC ERGO representative, which keeps it clear of mis-selling."

4 From question to proposal 3 min

I want to buy this for my shop. Should I send you my PAN and Aadhaar here?

It says not to share them in chat and points to the Apply tab. Open Apply, choose Named Perils, tick Section II and Section XI, fill in a name and mobile, and download the filled HDFC ERGO proposal form. Click Send to the assistant to have it confirm the choices, then Submit to HDFC ERGO (demo).

Switch to the HDFC ERGO Desk: the proposal is waiting. Click Approve and issue policy; a policy number is issued and the person appears on the People page with a copy card.

One flow covers the whole journey: question, filled form, HDFC ERGO decision and a live policy.

5 The claim at 11 pm (the main moment) 5 min

Click New chat. Play Ramesh:

Someone broke into my shop last night and took jewellery

It first checks everyone is safe and asks if there is a policy. Then:

Yes. VS-2026-10001, mobile 9829000001 123456 Gold jewellery worth about 3 lakh was taken from the display counter, last night.

It says likely covered, that HDFC ERGO's surveyor decides, what to do now (FIR, don't clear the scene, keep bills) and asks to register.

Yes, register it

It gives a reference like CLM-2026-0412. Switch to the Desk: the claim is there with the reasons and the customer's own words. Change its status to Surveyor assigned. Back in the chat:

What is the status of my claim?
The assistant reports "Surveyor assigned" and the next step. The customer and HDFC ERGO see the same data, live.

6 It's honest when the answer is no 2 min

New chat, as Sunita (VS-2026-10002, 9835000002, OTP 123456):

Heavy rain water entered my shop and all my stock is spoiled

Likely not covered, because her Named Perils policy has no storm and flood add-on, with the page reference. It still offers to register the claim.

"It never says approved or rejected and never promises an amount. It tells the customer the likely position and why, and reporting the loss is always their right."

7 Talk to it 3 min

Click the headset button in the chat (Start a live voice conversation). Speak in Hinglish, for example: "Namaste, mere dukaan mein kal raat chori ho gayi." Give the policy number and mobile digit by digit; "double zero" works. Say the OTP as "one two three four five six" or "ek do teen char paanch chhe".

Use earphones, speak a beat after it finishes and keep the room quiet. If it hears nothing, stop the call, check the mic, start again.

8 Let them try it 2 min, optional

On People, click + Add a person and use a name someone in the room suggests, with any 10-digit mobile and a status chip (Active, Expiring, Expired, Upcoming). Hand them the laptop: they copy the policy number from the new card and make a claim themselves.

Nothing is scripted when the audience makes up the customer. This convinces people more than anything else.

"No policy matches"Copy the number from the People page; check the mobile matches that card.

Voice doesn't hear youEarphones, Chrome mic permission, Windows mic at 100%. Or switch to chat; the story is the same.

Questions HDFC ERGO may ask

QuestionAnswer
Can it make things up?Policy answers come only from HDFC ERGO's four documents, with page references. Whether a claim is likely covered is decided by fixed rules in code (policy period, add-ons, sections taken, exclusions, the 30-day reporting rule, the empty-premises rule), not by the AI. The AI only explains the result.
Does it handle personal data?It never asks for PAN, Aadhaar, KYC or bank details and tells customers to give them directly to HDFC ERGO. Policy details are shown only after OTP verification.
How does it connect to our systems?Through seven tools: verify, OTP, get policy, check claim, register claim, claim status and call-back. In production these call HDFC ERGO's policy admin and claims systems instead of the demo store; the assistant doesn't change.
Can it approve or reject a claim?No. It says "likely covered" or "likely not covered" and that HDFC ERGO's surveyor decides. It never promises an amount.
Which languages?English, Hinglish and Hindi in chat and voice today. More Indian languages can be added.
Can it do outbound calls, like renewal reminders?Yes; that's the natural next step. See "What to add next".

Fix in Perfox before the demo

  1. Reply length: open the agent, click the AI Agent node, and raise the maximum output or max tokens setting (to about 1,500). Short limits cut answers off mid-sentence.
  2. Paste the updated prompts: both now include the 21 numbered sections, so the assistant can't mix up section numbers. Chat: Bot Instructions.md. Voice: Bot Instructions - Voice.md. Publish after pasting.
  3. Name in the chat header: Appearance, set the display name to Suraksha Sahayak (it shows "Perfox" now).
  4. Phone numbers: the privacy filter hides 10-digit numbers, so the WhatsApp contact 8169 500 500 is masked. Allow phone numbers in the privacy settings if you can.
