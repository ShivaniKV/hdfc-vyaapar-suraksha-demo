Vyaapar Suraksha Assistant: Demo Script

About 15 minutes · One page, just the chat · hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev · All customers and data are made up

The page has only a heading and the chat, the way the assistant would sit on HDFC ERGO's own website. Everything happens in the chat. It does two jobs: answer policy questions, and help with a loss, whether or not the person has a policy.

Before you start (5 minutes)

  • Perfox: the latest Common and Web Voice prompts are pasted (clear each box first), tools refreshed (submit_proposal is gone, request_application is new), agent Published, Rich replies = Text only, credits available.
  • Open hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev/?reset in Chrome. It clears old claims and the chat, then reloads as the normal page. The audience never sees a reset button.
  • Send one warm-up question (What is Section I?), then click New chat (icon at the top of the chat).
  • For voice: earphones with a mic, Chrome mic allowed, quiet room.
  • Keep this script open on your own screen; the people's details are below.

Part 1 · Policy questions (4 min)

1 The problem 1 min

Say: "21 sections and a 100-page wording. A shopkeeper can't read that, and no agent is awake at 11 pm. This assistant sits on HDFC ERGO's site, explains the policy and handles the claim, in chat or voice, in English, Hinglish and Hindi."

2 A question with its source Chat 2 min

What is not covered under burglary? I run a mobile phone shop.
Only the exclusions that matter to a phone shop, ending with a Source line. Click it: HDFC ERGO's PDF opens at that page in a new tab.
Every answer traces back to a page in HDFC ERGO's own documents.

3 Their language, and no selling Chat 1 min

मेरी किराने की दुकान है। बाढ़ से स्टॉक खराब हो जाए तो क्या कवर मिलेगा?

It answers in Hindi. Then in Hinglish:

Mujhe kaunsa section lena chahiye? Aap hi bata do best kya hai
It explains the options but won't choose for the customer. No mis-selling.

Part 2 · A loss, but no policy (3 min)

Click New chat first.

4 "My shop burnt" Chat 1 min

My shop caught fire last night and a lot of stock is burnt. What do I do?
Asks if everyone is safe and whether you have a Vyaapar Suraksha policy.
Everyone is fine. No, I don't have any policy.
Says kindly there is no claim to make, and that a policy bought now can't pay for a loss that already happened. Then: Vyaapar Suraksha can protect the shop from now on. Want to know more, or have HDFC ERGO call you to apply?
Honest, never mis-sells, and still helpful: a customer with no claim becomes a lead.

5 Apply through the chat Chat 2 min

Yes, I want to apply.
Asks only name, mobile, type of business and city. Never PAN, Aadhaar or bank details.
Arjun Mehta, 9000011111, hardware shop in Nagpur
Reads it back in one line and asks to confirm. Say yes → reference APP-2026-0101. An HDFC ERGO representative will call the mobile ending 1111 to finish the application; no cover until HDFC ERGO accepts it and the premium is paid.
No forms on the page. HDFC ERGO's team gets a ready lead and does the rest in their own system.

If they ask

QuestionAnswer
Where do the policy and claim data come from?Here, a small stand-in service with made-up customers. On HDFC ERGO's site, the same tools connect to HDFC ERGO's own policy, OTP and claims systems.
What happens after a claim is registered?HDFC ERGO's claims team picks it up in their own system, calls the customer and sends a surveyor. The assistant can still confirm it's registered and book a call-back.
Who decides cover?The tools apply the policy's rules to what the customer bought; the assistant only explains. It always says "likely", and the surveyor decides.
Does it collect PAN or bank details?Never. An application is just name, mobile, business and city; HDFC ERGO collects the rest when they call.
Languages?English, Hinglish and Hindi in chat and voice; more can be added.
The earlier version with forms and a desk?Saved, and live at hdfc-vs-full.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev if it's ever needed.
