Vyaapar Suraksha Assistant: Demo Script

About 15 minutes · One page, just the chat · hdfc-vs-demo.skillmine-hr-api.workers.dev · All customers and data are made up

The page has only a heading and the chat, the way the assistant would sit on HDFC ERGO's own website. It does two jobs: answer policy questions, and help with a loss, whether or not the person has a policy.

How to read this script: yellow is what you say to the audience · grey is what you type in the chat, the way a real shopkeeper would · blue is what the assistant should reply · green is the point to land.

Before you start (5 minutes)

  • For voice: earphones with a mic, Chrome mic allowed, quiet room.

Opening (1 min)

You say: "A small shopkeeper buys Vyaapar Suraksha, but the policy has 21 sections and a 100-page wording. When something goes wrong, at 11 at night, there's nobody to ask. This assistant sits on HDFC ERGO's own website. It does two things: it explains the policy in simple words, and when there's a loss, it tells the customer honestly whether it's likely covered and registers the claim. In English, Hindi or Hinglish, by chat or by voice. Let me show you."

Part 1 · Policy questions (3 min)

1 A simple question 1 min

You say: "First, a shopkeeper who just wants to understand the policy. No login needed."

I have a mobile shop. If someone steals phones at night, will I get money?
A short answer in plain words, with what is and isn't covered, ending with a Source line. Click it: HDFC ERGO's PDF opens at that exact page.

You say: "Every answer comes from HDFC ERGO's own documents, and it shows the page. It doesn't make things up."

2 Their language 1 min

You say: "Most of these customers are more comfortable in Hindi or Hinglish."
